How does this rule engine work?

The detector evaluates your statement against three heuristic layers:

  • Sensational Red Flags (+1 Suspicion each): Common clickbait terms like shocking, miracle, exposed, 100% cure, doctors hate, banned, secret, urgent, etc.
  • Authoritative Citations (+1 Credibility each): Formal institutional identifiers like according to, official, ministry, report, confirmed, study, police, university, etc.
  • Stylistic Flags (+1 Suspicion each): Excessive punctuation (!! or ??) and heavy capitalization (>70% uppercase).
