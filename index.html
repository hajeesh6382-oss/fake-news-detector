      About the Veritas AI Verification Engine

      Architecture & Ethics

      1. Mission Statement

      Misinformation erodes democratic institutions and public health. Veritas AI was built to empower researchers, journalists, and citizens to rapidly interrogate news articles, YouTube transcripts, and viral social media forwards against verifiable evidence without relying on black-box guesswork.

      2. Core Principles of Verification

      • Zero Simulated Results: Every evidence link, quotation, and fact-check card corresponds to a real, accessible database record or live article.
      • Absence of Evidence is Not Evidence of Absence: When a claim cannot be verified because no outlet has yet reported on it, our system labels it UNVERIFIED, strictly avoiding false "Fake" classifications.
      • Temporal Auditing: The engine inspects publication dates to prevent recycled historical events from deceiving readers as fresh breaking developments.
      • Headline Divergence Detection: NLP measures the semantic alignment between a headline and the article body to flag deceptive clickbait.

      3. Architecture Stack

      Python 3.14 FastAPI SQLite & SQLAlchemy Google Fact Check API YouTube Transcript API BeautifulSoup4 Natural Language Processing SSRF Security Shield Vanilla JS (Zero Heavy Frameworks)
